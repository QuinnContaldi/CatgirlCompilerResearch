## Screen text

CONSENT TO PARTICIPATE IN RESEARCH
Programming-Language Study

Please read this information before deciding whether to participate. You may ask the researcher questions before you begin.

KEY INFORMATION
You are invited to take part in research about how people experience different interfaces for a programming-language study. Participation is voluntary. [RESEARCHER TO COMPLETE: Expected duration, confirmed in the two-condition pilot.] We expect little risk, mainly temporary frustration or tiredness. There is no expected direct benefit to you. [RESEARCHER TO COMPLETE: Payment, course credit, other compensation, or no compensation, consistent with recruitment materials.] Your responses may contribute to understanding how people experience programming-language studies. You may choose not to participate or stop at any time without penalty or loss of benefits to which you are otherwise entitled.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
You will read a brief overview and may describe your programming background. You will then complete two sections of four short programming questions, selecting one answer per question. The sections use different interface presentations. After each section, you will answer eight rating items about the study interface. At the end, you will choose which format you would prefer for a future similar study and may explain your choice in your own words. The app does not provide hints or tell you whether your answers are correct. The study content is prepared in advance; the study does not send your responses to a live AI service.

RISKS AND DISCOMFORTS
The activities are expected to involve risks similar to ordinary computer-based reading and problem solving. You may experience mild frustration, boredom, fatigue, or eye strain. There is also a possible risk of loss of privacy if research records are accessed without authorization. You may pause or stop if you feel uncomfortable. Tell the researcher if you have a concern or experience a problem.

BENEFITS AND PAYMENT
You should not expect a direct personal benefit. [RESEARCHER TO COMPLETE: Confirm compensation arrangements, including any course-credit alternative if applicable.] The possible benefit is contributing to research on how programming-language studies are presented and experienced.

YOUR CHOICE
Taking part is your choice. You may decline by selecting the return-to-setup button instead of Accept. You may stop later by telling the researcher or closing the study. Programming-background information and the final written explanation are optional. Programming questions, interface ratings, and the preference choice require an answer to advance; if you do not want to answer, you may stop rather than continue. You do not have to give a reason. Responses already submitted are saved as you go.

INFORMATION RECORDED AND PRIVACY
The app assigns a random participant ID and does not ask for your name. It records any programming-background information you provide, the assigned interface and task order, your selected answers, whether each answer is correct, time spent on each programming question, questionnaire ratings, your preferred study format, and any written explanation you provide. It also records your consent decision and its time. Please do not include your name or other identifying details in written responses.

Records are saved locally on the study computer. The app does not automatically upload them. A random ID reduces identification risk but does not guarantee complete anonymity, especially if you include identifying information in a written response.

QUESTIONS AND CONTACTS
For questions about the study, or to report a study-related problem or harm, contact:
[RESEARCHER NAME, INSTITUTION, AND EMAIL OR PHONE]

YOUR CONSENT
By selecting Accept, you indicate that you have read this information, have had an opportunity to ask questions, and voluntarily agree to participate. Selecting Accept does not waive any of your legal rights. If you do not wish to participate, do not select Accept.
