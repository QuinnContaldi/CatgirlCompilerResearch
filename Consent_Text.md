# Compiler Feedback Study — draft consent text

The text below is installed in `UnityProject/Assets/Data/StudyDefinition.asset`
as **Consent Text**. The existing consent screen displays it; acceptance saves
its exact wording and a timestamp in the participant session.

This is a draft based on standard informed-consent elements, not an IRB approval
or a determination that the study is exempt or minimal risk. Before participant
recruitment, replace the bracketed contact and data-policy fields and use the
wording and consent process required by your institution. The draft's 12–18
minute estimate comes from `Research_Design_Document.pdf` and README.md; confirm
it in a pilot. No payment is stated following the requested no-reward description.
Confirm that this matches recruitment arrangements, including any course credit.
Do not promise that a participant can skip a required questionnaire and continue:
the current app permits stopping but requires answers to advance on those pages.
Confirm with your institution whether this click-to-accept process is appropriate;
the app does not collect a signature. Provide the finalized information in a form
participants can keep using the study's approved process.

Authoritative references:
- [HHS OHRP informed-consent FAQs](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/guidance/faq/informed-consent/index.html)
- [Revised Common Rule, 45 CFR 46.116 and 46.117](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/sites/default/files/revised-common-rule-reg-text-unofficial-2018-requirements.pdf)

## Screen text

CONSENT TO PARTICIPATE IN RESEARCH
Compiler Feedback Study

Please read this information before deciding whether to participate. You may ask the researcher questions before you begin.

KEY INFORMATION
You are invited to take part in research about how people experience different styles of programming-error feedback. Participation is voluntary and is expected to take approximately 12–18 minutes, depending on your reading speed. We expect little risk, mainly temporary frustration or tiredness. There is no expected direct benefit to you and no payment for participating. Your responses may contribute to scientific understanding of programming support tools. You may choose not to participate or stop at any time without penalty or loss of benefits to which you are otherwise entitled.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
You will read brief instructions, review six short programming-error scenarios presented with different feedback styles, and select an answer for each scenario. After each pair of scenarios, you will answer a short questionnaire about the feedback. You will also answer questions about your experience with the assistant, choose a preferred feedback style, and have an opportunity to explain your preference in your own words. The feedback is prepared in advance; the study does not send your responses to a live AI service.

RISKS AND DISCOMFORTS
The activities are expected to involve risks similar to ordinary computer-based reading and problem solving. You may experience mild frustration, boredom, fatigue, or eye strain. There is also a possible risk of loss of privacy if research records are accessed without authorization. You may pause or stop if you feel uncomfortable. Tell the researcher if you have a concern or experience a problem.

BENEFITS AND PAYMENT
You should not expect a personal benefit, payment, or other reward. The possible benefit is contributing to research on how programming feedback is presented and experienced.

YOUR CHOICE
Taking part is your choice. You may decline by selecting the return-to-setup button instead of Accept. You may stop later by telling the researcher or closing the study. Some questions require an answer to advance; if you do not want to answer, you may stop rather than continue. You do not have to give a reason. Responses already submitted are saved as you go.

INFORMATION RECORDED AND PRIVACY
The app assigns a random participant ID and does not ask for your name. It records the assigned feedback and task order, your selected answers, whether each answer is correct, time spent on each programming question, questionnaire ratings, your preferred feedback style, and any written explanation you provide. It also records your consent decision and its time. Please do not include your name or other identifying details in written responses.

Records are saved locally on the study computer. The app does not automatically upload them. A random ID reduces identification risk but does not guarantee complete anonymity, especially if you include identifying information in a written response.

For questions about the study, or to report a study-related problem or harm, contact:
[RESEARCHER NAME, INSTITUTION, AND EMAIL OR PHONE]

[INSTITUTIONAL IRB / RESEARCH ETHICS OFFICE AND EMAIL OR PHONE]

YOUR CONSENT
By selecting Accept, you indicate that you have read this information, have had an opportunity to ask questions, and voluntarily agree to participate. Selecting Accept does not waive any of your legal rights. If you do not wish to participate, do not select Accept.
